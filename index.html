MCB marks updater

Fill the marks for a whole class in one sheet, one tab per section. Then upload them to MyClassBoard in one go.

New, and not yet proven on real marks. The first time, use "Tick only the first section", upload that one section, and look at it in MyClassBoard before you upload the rest.

How to use it

  1. Add the bookmark (one time) Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B), then drag this button onto it:
    MCB marks updater
    Drag it, do not click it here. It only works on a MyClassBoard page.
  2. Open it inside MyClassBoard Log in to MyClassBoard on a laptop or desktop as usual, then click the bookmark. A panel opens on the right side of the page.
  3. Make the template Choose the class, the examination and the subject. If the subject has parts, type them, for example Physics 7, Chemistry 6, Biology 7. You get one Excel file with a tab for every section, with each student's enrollment code and name already filled in. Upload it to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets.
  4. Fill in the marks Type the marks for each part. The total is added up for you. For an absent student type A, AB, L or AM. Leave a student blank to enter them later.
  5. Upload Back in MyClassBoard, click the bookmark, open Upload marks, and give it the sheet: the downloaded .xlsx file, or the Google Sheets link. Review what will be written for each section, then press Upload. Each section is read back from MyClassBoard afterwards and compared with the sheet.

What it does with your data

  • It runs in your own browser, inside your own MyClassBoard login. It never sees your password.
  • Marks go from your sheet to MyClassBoard and nowhere else. Nothing is stored or sent to this site.
  • A Google Sheets link only works if the sheet is shared as "Anyone with the link", which lets anyone holding the link read the marks. To keep the sheet private, use File → Download → Microsoft Excel and choose that file instead.
  • Nothing is written until you have seen the preview and pressed Upload. A section with a mistake is skipped whole.
